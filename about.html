About the Science Canteen

Your meeting place between lectures, labs and study sessions

Who We Are

The Science Canteen is the main food outlet for students and staff of the Faculty of Science, University of Ruhuna. Located within the Faculty premises in Matara, it provides meals, snacks and beverages throughout the working day so that students can stay energised for lectures, practicals and group studies.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide fresh, hygienic and affordable food to the Faculty of Science community in a clean and welcoming environment.

👁️ Our Vision

To be a friendly and trusted canteen that supports the health and well-being of every student and staff member.
